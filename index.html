Find a laundry. Order. Track. Get it delivered.

Laundry Hub brings many laundries in Addis Ababa into one system. Customers choose a laundry and order online, laundry owners run their own shop, and drivers pick up and deliver the clothes.

[ Browse laundries ] [ Login ]

How it works

1. ChooseThe customer looks at the list of laundries, their prices and their ratings, and picks one.
2. OrderThe customer lists the clothes and chooses drop-off or home pickup, and collect or delivery.
3. WashThe laundry accepts the order and moves it through received, washing, finished and ready.
4. DeliverA Laundry Hub driver picks up the clothes and brings them back. Payment is recorded on delivery.

Who uses the system

RoleWhat they do
CustomerRegisters, browses laundries, orders, tracks the order, rates the laundry
Laundry ownerRegisters a laundry, sets prices, adds staff and drivers, accepts orders, sees reports
ShopkeeperRegisters walk-in orders, updates status, hands over clothes and records payment
DriverRegisters once, is approved by the admin, then takes pickup and delivery jobs from any laundry
AdminApproves new laundries and manages users
